## World Atlas / Natural Earth boundary data

The projection comparison maps in `public/images/map-inquiry/` are generated from
`scripts/data/world-atlas-110m.json`, sourced from the `world-atlas` project and
derived from Natural Earth 1:110m data.

- World Atlas: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Natural Earth data is public domain. The World Atlas package is distributed under
the ISC licence. The generated projection styling and lesson annotations are
original to this project.
