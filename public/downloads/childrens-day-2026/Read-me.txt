PRINTING
Print PDF at 100% or Fit. The poster is page 1, within printer-safe margins.
Pages 2-6 use light fills and work in greyscale. Print single-sided for drawing and writing, or select only the pages needed.
K-3 activity: page 3. Grades 4-7: page 4. Leadership / Spirit Crew: page 5, with adult follow-through on page 6.
The PDF is a print worksheet, not a fillable or tagged-accessibility PDF. Use the text companion to enlarge, adapt or read the contents aloud.

USE
Choose activities locally. No school-wide assembly, collection, deadline or project has been scheduled or approved by this resource.
Before collecting student ideas, an adult sponsor should confirm privacy arrangements, scope and a response process.
Do not collect pupil names, photos or private experiences. Participation and sharing are optional.
The classroom theme and activities are original, not the official 2026 campaign theme. Rights are paraphrases, not the full Convention.
